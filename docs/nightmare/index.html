The boss drops 1 to 4 uber fragments, each one picked at random from its area's pool. ×1 … ×4 is the average value of that many fragments; Expected weighs them by the chances above (even odds by default, as the real ones aren't known). Profit takes off the Nightmare Map's cost, and P(profit) is the chance the fragments alone are worth more than the map. The uniques and exceptional gems the area also drops aren't counted, so real returns are a bit higher. The last row is the average over the five areas, for when you can't pick the area. Prices are poe.ninja mid values; thin marks fragments trading under 20k chaos a day.
