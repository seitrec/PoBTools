Prices are poe.ninja's listing-based estimates, so a viscera with few listings (thin, under 10) can be far off. The same viscera can appear several times, once per mod, at very different prices. Source comes from poedb: map: the Greater version drops in the map named next to it, the Lesser and plain ones in the campaign zone named next to them; extra is a Greater version that no map drops, so it needs a random extra encounter; boss drops from the boss itself, and uber is the Greater version from the uber boss. The arrow after a map is its quadrant of the atlas (↖ TL top left, ↗ TR, ↙ BL, ↘ BR), from poedb's atlas. Change is poe.ninja's price change over the last 7 days. Coven Queen's Crown is left out: it has hundreds of versions, one per mod.
