Tiers

Avg outcome is the mean price of the tier's essences, what a swap pays on average from the cheapest input. Best reroll start plays the tier optimally: swap, keep any result marked keep (worth more than swapping it again), otherwise swap again. Its profit is per starting essence, after all swaps.

Per essence

A swap turns an essence into a different one of the same tier, each equally likely (the essence never comes back as itself). P(profit) is the chance one swap gives an essence worth more than the input plus the swap cost; EV is the average gain of one swap, flat and as % of what you put in (input price + swap). Rerolled is the gain when you keep swapping until you land on a keep essence. Prices are poe.ninja mid values: buying and selling eat a spread, and low-volume prices (thin, under 100c/day) are unreliable.
